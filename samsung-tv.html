Nagenli for Samsung TV

At the end, Nagenli is an app on your Samsung TV. It is a one-time install: put the TV in Developer Mode, then run the installer. For Samsung smart TVs from 2019 and newer.

About 10 minutes

You need

  • A Samsung smart TV from 2019 or newer, and its remote
  • A Windows PC on the same home network as the TV
  • Nagenli already installed on a computer (this one or another)
  • The person who invited you, a message away, in case your TV needs something extra

Install Nagenli on your computer first (Windows, Mac or Linux). This page installs the TV app from a Windows PC. Only a Mac in the house? Use the Mac page.

Part 1: get the kit

  1. Download the kit

    Download Nagenli-TV-Kit.zipfor Samsung TV, from a Windows PCabout 2 MB

    You will seeNagenli-TV-Kit.zip in your browser's list of downloads.

  2. Extract the whole zip to a folder

    Right-click the file and choose Extract All.

    You will seeA new folder with Install Nagenli on TV in it.

  3. Run Install Nagenli on TV

    Double-click Install Nagenli on TV in the new folder.

    You will seeA window. Its first line is this computer's address, for example 192.168.1.20.

    If this happens

    A blue window says “Windows protected your PC”?

    Windows shows this for programs it does not know yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

  4. Write down the address, then close the window

    You need the address in the next part. You can close the window for now.

Part 2: Developer Mode on the TV

  1. On the TV, open Apps

    Press the Home button on the remote and choose Apps.

    You will seeThe Apps screen.

  2. Type 1 2 3 4 5 on the remote

    With the Apps screen open, press 1 2 3 4 5 on the remote. On a remote without digits: press the 123 button and pick the digits on screen.

    You will seeA window called Developer Mode opens.

    If this happens

    Nothing opens?

    Make sure the Apps screen is open, and press the digits one after another without waiting.

  3. Turn Developer Mode on and type the address

    In the window: set Developer Mode to On, and in Host PC IP type the computer's address from the earlier step. If the menus of the TV are in a right-to-left language, the four boxes fill from the right: check that it reads the same as on the computer, box by box. Choose OK.

  4. Restart the TV

    Hold the power button on the remote until the TV turns off and on again.

    You will seeThe top of the Apps screen says Developer Mode.

Part 3: install

  1. Run Install Nagenli on TV again

    Double-click it on the computer again. It finds the TV by itself and installs Nagenli.

    You will seeNagenli opens on the TV.

    If this happens

    It says it cannot find the TV?

    Check that the TV is on, that Developer Mode is on (the top of the Apps screen says Developer Mode), and that the address you typed on the TV is this computer's.

    If this happens

    The TV refused the app?

    Some Samsung TVs take only apps signed for that one TV. The TV's code was copied: paste it in a message to the person who invited you, and they will tell you what to do.

Afterwards

Nagenli is in Apps like any other app. It updates by itself through your computer; there is nothing to install again.

Watching away from home

Optional. In another home (a holiday flat, a relative's) the TV can reach your computer over the internet.

  1. On your computer, switch on Watch from anywhere

    Open Nagenli's Settings › Devices and access and switch Watch from anywhere on.

    You will seeWait until it says Ready. Under it is a connect code, like K7Q-4M2-X9T.

  2. On the TV, open Nagenli

    When it cannot find your computer, it shows a picture code (a QR).

    You will seeA picture code and the words Scan with your phone's camera to connect this TV to your Nagenli.

  3. Scan it with a phone that is already connected to Nagenli

    Open the phone's camera, point it at the TV, tap the link and choose Connect in Nagenli. Nothing is typed on the TV.

    You will seeThe TV connects and remembers itself.

  4. No phone? Type the connect code instead

    Type the connect code (capitals or not, dashes or not) and press OK. The TV then asks for the six-digit pairing code, shown in Nagenli's Settings › Devices and access on your computer (it also shows a picture code to scan). Type it once; the TV is remembered.

Your router has to pass port 443 to the computer (Nagenli asks it to, and most routers agree). If Watch from anywhere shows another port, the phone app can still connect but a TV cannot.

When your internet provider changes your home's address, the TV looks the connect code up again and carries on by itself. If you press New code in Nagenli's Settings, scan the TV's picture code again, or type the new code on the TV.

Stuck? That is fine, it happens

The installer cannot find the TV

Check that the TV is on, that Developer Mode is on (the top of the Apps screen says Developer Mode), and that the address you typed on the TV is this computer's. The TV and the computer must be on the same home network.

The TV did not let the computer in

Restart the TV fully: hold the power button on the remote until the Samsung logo shows. Check that the address in Host PC IP is exactly this computer's, that no VPN is on, and that the computer uses one network connection only (Wi-Fi or cable, not both). Then run the installer again.

The TV refused the app

Some Samsung TVs take only apps signed for that one TV. The installer shows what the TV answered and copies the TV's code and model. Paste them in a message to the person who invited you, and they will tell you what to do.

Typing 1 2 3 4 5 opens nothing

Make sure the Apps screen is open and press the digits one after another. On a remote without digits, press the 123 button and pick the digits on screen.

Ask for a hand

Nothing here helped? Send a message to the person who invited you to Nagenli. Tell them which step you are on, and add a photo of the screen (of the TV, if it is the TV that is stuck; a phone photo of a TV is fine).
