Nagenli for Mac

This is the computer that holds your films and series. At the end, Nagenli runs on your Mac and everything is ready to watch. You install once with one line, and Nagenli keeps itself up to date. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

About 5 minutes

You need

  • A Mac that holds your films and series, and can stay on and awake while others watch
  • The internet, for the first install
  • The folders where your films and series are kept

Install

  1. Open Terminal

    Press Cmd + Space, type Terminal, and press Enter. Terminal is a program every Mac has; it lets you paste one line.

    You will seeA window with a blinking cursor, waiting for you.

  2. Paste the line and press Enter

    Press the big Copy button, click inside the Terminal window, press Cmd + V, then Enter.

    Paste this line into Terminal

    curl -fsSL https://quantumleapinco.github.io/nagenli-releases/install-mac.sh | bash

    You will seeText scrolls by while Nagenli is put on your Mac, then Nagenli opens in your browser.

  3. Say Allow if macOS asks about incoming connections

    If macOS asks whether “nagenli” may accept incoming connections, click Allow. Without it your phone and TV cannot find this Mac.

    You will seeThe question closes. If it never appears, that is fine: carry on.

  4. Choose your folders in Nagenli

    Choose the folders that hold your films and series. When macOS asks to allow access to a folder or an external drive, click Allow.

    You will seeYour films and series on the screen.

Nagenli only reads your folders. It never changes, moves or deletes anything in them.

Afterwards

Where Nagenli lives

In a browser on this Mac, Nagenli is at the address below. Bookmark it: there is no menu bar icon on a Mac.

This Mac's own address for Nagenli

http://localhost:8686

It starts with the Mac. The Mac has to be on and awake for other devices to watch.

Updates

Automatic, a few times a day, and only while nobody is watching. To check by hand: in Nagenli, Settings › Help and about › Check for updates.

Your other devices

This Mac's address on your home network is shown in Nagenli under Settings, for example http://192.168.1.20:8686. You need it to install on an Android TV.

Watching away from home

Optional. It lets a phone or TV in another home reach this Mac.

  1. Switch on Watch from anywhere

    In Nagenli on this computer, open Settings › Devices and access and switch Watch from anywhere on.

    You will seeIt says setting up, then Ready. Under it is a connect code, like K7Q-4M2-X9T.

  2. Type the code on the other device

    On a TV or phone in another home, type the connect code, then the pairing code. It leads only to this Mac.

Remove Nagenli

Only if you want Nagenli off this Mac. Paste this line in Terminal, as before.

Paste this line into Terminal to remove Nagenli

curl -fsSL https://quantumleapinco.github.io/nagenli-releases/uninstall-mac.sh | bash

Stuck? That is fine, it happens

Nothing happened after I pressed Enter

Make sure the whole line was pasted: it starts with curl and ends with bash. Click inside the Terminal window and paste again with Cmd + V. The first time it needs the internet and may take a minute.

I closed Nagenli in the browser. How do I open it again?

Open a browser and go to http://localhost:8686. Nagenli keeps running in the background, and it starts with the Mac.

My phone or TV cannot find this Mac

Check three things: the Mac is on and awake; the phone or TV is on the same home Wi-Fi; and you clicked Allow when macOS asked about incoming connections. If you clicked Don't Allow, tell the person who invited you.
