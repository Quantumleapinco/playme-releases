Nagenli for Samsung TV, from a Mac

At the end, Nagenli is an app on your Samsung TV. It is a one-time install: put the TV in Developer Mode, then run the installer. For Samsung smart TVs from 2019 and newer.

About 10 minutes

You need

  • A Samsung smart TV from 2019 or newer, and its remote
  • A Mac on the same home network as the TV
  • Nagenli already installed on a computer (this Mac or another)
  • The person who invited you, a message away, in case your TV needs something extra

The Mac is only the tool that puts the app on the TV. Your Nagenli server can be this Mac, another computer, or in another home.

Part 1: get the Mac ready

  1. Open Terminal

    Press Cmd + Space, type Terminal, and press Enter.

    You will seeA window with a blinking cursor, waiting for you.

  2. Only on a Mac with an Apple chip: add Rosetta

    Samsung's TV tool needs Apple's Rosetta on Macs with an Apple chip (M1 and newer). Not sure? Click the Apple menu, then About This Mac: a Mac with an Apple chip says “Chip: Apple M…”. On an Intel Mac, skip this step. Otherwise paste this line and press Enter; it takes a minute or two.

    Paste this line into Terminal (Apple chip only)

    softwareupdate --install-rosetta --agree-to-license

    You will seeText scrolls by, then the cursor comes back. If Rosetta is already there it says so, and that is fine.

  3. Paste the TV line and press Enter

    Paste this line into Terminal

    curl -fsSL https://quantumleapinco.github.io/nagenli-releases/install-tv-mac.sh | bash

    You will seeThe first line it prints is this Mac's address, for example 192.168.1.20. It will then say that the TV did not answer or did not let the Mac in: that is expected until the TV is in Developer Mode.

  4. Write down the address

    You need it in the next part.

Part 2: Developer Mode on the TV

  1. On the TV, open Apps

    Press the Home button on the remote and choose Apps.

    You will seeThe Apps screen.

  2. Type 1 2 3 4 5 on the remote

    With the Apps screen open, press 1 2 3 4 5 on the remote. On a remote without digits: press the 123 button and pick the digits on screen.

    You will seeA window called Developer Mode opens.

    If this happens

    Nothing opens?

    Make sure the Apps screen is open, and press the digits one after another without waiting.

  3. Turn Developer Mode on and type the address

    In the window: set Developer Mode to On, and in Host PC IP type the Mac's address from the earlier step. If the menus of the TV are in a right-to-left language, the four boxes fill from the right: check that it reads the same as on the computer, box by box. Choose OK.

  4. Restart the TV

    Hold the power button on the remote until the TV turns off and on again.

    You will seeThe top of the Apps screen says Developer Mode.

Part 3: install

  1. Run the same line again

    In Terminal, press the Up arrow and then Enter. It finds the TV by itself and installs Nagenli.

    You will seeNagenli opens on the TV.

    If this happens

    It says it cannot find the TV?

    Check that the TV is on, that Developer Mode is on (the top of the Apps screen says Developer Mode), and that the address you typed on the TV is this Mac's.

    If this happens

    The TV refused the app?

    Some Samsung TVs take only apps signed for that one TV. The TV's code was copied: paste it in a message to the person who invited you, and they will tell you what to do.

Connecting the TV to Nagenli

If your Nagenli is in the same home, the TV finds it by itself. If it is somewhere else, the TV shows a picture code: on a phone that is already connected to Nagenli, open the camera, point it at the TV, tap the link and choose Connect in Nagenli. Or type the connect code shown in Nagenli's Settings on the computer, under Watch from anywhere, then the pairing code.

Afterwards

Nagenli is in Apps like any other app. It updates by itself through your computer; there is nothing to install again.

What the line does: it downloads Samsung's own TV tool and the Nagenli TV app into a small folder of its own, and changes nothing else on the Mac.

Stuck? That is fine, it happens

It says the TV did not answer

Before part 2 this is expected. After part 2, check that the TV is on, that Developer Mode is on (the top of the Apps screen says Developer Mode), and that the address you typed on the TV is this Mac's. The TV and the Mac must be on the same home network.

The TV did not let the Mac in

Restart the TV fully: hold the power button on the remote until the Samsung logo shows. Check that the address in Host PC IP is exactly this Mac's, that no VPN is on, and that the Mac uses one network connection only (Wi-Fi or cable, not both). On a Mac, also open System Settings, Privacy & Security, Local Network, and switch Terminal on (if it is on, switch it off and on). Then quit Terminal, open it again, and run the line again.

The TV refused the app

Some Samsung TVs take only apps signed for that one TV. The line shows what the TV answered and copies the TV's code and model. Paste them in a message to the person who invited you, and they will tell you what to do.

Rosetta, or a message I do not understand

Only Macs with an Apple chip need Rosetta. If the line for Rosetta says it is already installed, that is fine. For any other message, take a screenshot of the Terminal window (Cmd + Shift + 4) and send it to the person who invited you.

Ask for a hand

Nothing here helped? Send a message to the person who invited you to Nagenli. Tell them which step you are on, and add a photo of the screen (of the TV, if it is the TV that is stuck; a phone photo of a TV is fine).
